  2. Metrological methodology

    A measuring artifact for point picking accuracy

    A metrological methodology to measure how accurately an Augmented Reality system tracks the tip of a surgical tool, with a dedicated 3D printed artifact the tooltip is positioned on during the point picking measurements.

    Computers in Industry 2026

    3D preview, loading
    Planar horizontal divots
    Tilted plane divots
    Convex surface
    Concave surface
    Download model, 2.1 MB

    The 3D printed measuring artifact of the methodology, the instrument the tooltip is positioned on during the point picking measurements.

    1. 01

      The question

      Tracking errors in Augmented Reality propagate to every point picked with the tool.

      Measured in a relative way
      The collected points are aligned to their theoretical pattern, and that alignment compensates the absolute error away.
      No shared procedure
      No single repeatable procedure existed to compare different tracking methods.

    2. 02

      The measuring artifact

      Point picking is measured with a dedicated instrument of the same methodology, the 3D printed artifact shown beside these steps: the tooltip is positioned on the divots in turn, and the captured points are aligned to their theoretical pattern, so the same repeatable procedure can be run with any tracking method.

      Auxiliary reference
      A slot carries the reference system the captured points are aligned to.
      Qualified beforehand
      Divot centres are measured on a coordinate measuring system, and the tooltip is located by pivoting calibration.
      • 36hemispherical divots
      • 2.5 mmdivot radius
      • 200 mmsquare base

      Four kinds of surface

      • Planar horizontal
      • Planar tilted
      • Convex
      • Concave
