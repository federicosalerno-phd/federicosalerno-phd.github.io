Positions

  1. Since Nov 2025

    Postdoctoral Researcher

    DIGEP, Politecnico di Torino

    Research on markerless optical tracking for augmented surgical applications, gesture based interaction modeling, real time pose estimation, spatial registration robustness, and experimental validation protocols for AR guided procedures.

  2. Nov 2022 to Nov 2025

    PhD Student

    Politecnico di Torino, supervisor Prof. Sandro Moos

    PhD research in augmented reality for surgery. Design of AR solutions to register virtual holographic anatomical models onto the operating field. Study of 3D printed surgical cutting guides for osteotomies and AR assisted osteotomies. Development of methodologies to evaluate accuracy and precision of AR tracking systems, benchmarking of optical tracking solutions, and definition of an augmented surgical platform including an AR adjustable surgical cutting guide.

  3. Oct 2024 to Mar 2025

    Visiting PhD Student

    Technische Universität Wien, Vienna, Austria

    Research visit focused on augmented reality applications for surgical guidance.

  4. May 2022 to Nov 2022

    Research Fellowship

    Politecnico di Torino

    Dimensional and mechanical validation of surgical cutting guides. Geometric distortion analysis via 3D scanning of mandibular cutting guides. Mechanical strength assessment using FEM analysis and experimental tensile testing.

  5. May 2021 to Oct 2021

    CAD and CAM prototyping

    Dropper S.r.l., Torino

    CAD design of a people counter device casing.

  6. Nov 2019 to Sep 2021

    CAD and CAM prototyping

    Nova Stark S.r.l., Torino

    CAD design of a device casing for agritech viticulture environmental parameter measurement. Prototyping and experimental test bench for sensor testing.

  7. Apr 2021 to May 2021

    Video Lectures on Additive Manufacturing

    Sin Tesi Forma S.r.l., Torino

    Pre-recorded training course (3 hours) on additive manufacturing in the industry.

  8. Oct 2019 to Jan 2020

    Research Fellow, part time

    Politecnico di Torino

    Assistance during Industrial Technical Drawing lab sessions.
