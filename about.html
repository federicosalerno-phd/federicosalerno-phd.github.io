About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Politecnico di Torino. My research focuses on augmented and computer-assisted surgery, with applications in mixed reality, surgical navigation, 3D reconstruction, medical imaging, and system integration for biomedical contexts.

Research interests

  • Augmented surgery
  • Computer-assisted surgery
  • Mixed reality (AR/MR)
  • Surgical navigation
  • 3D reconstruction
  • Medical imaging
  • Optical tracking
  • Biomedical system integration
